## Rubric

|Criterion|Weight|Rating-3 evidence|
|---|---:|---|
|Integration/tool-platform delivery|30|Personally shipped/operated integration capability with named APIs/tools/clients, boundaries and consumer use. Internal production orchestration counts. MCP is one approach; prompting, RAG alone and coding-assistant usage do not establish integration ownership.|
|Backend/API/distributed-system depth|20|Concrete production backend/API implementation, relevant runtime-family evidence, data/async behavior, failure handling and personal scope. Frontend TypeScript or skills alone do not prove backend depth.|
|Third-party auth/security|20|Concrete integration identity/access implementation and boundaries, such as scopes/tenant isolation, token handling, secrets or least privilege appropriate to the system. JWT/login keywords alone are insufficient.|
|Reliability/production operations|15|Personally operated services with deployment/monitoring, incidents, safe migration or performance/resilience work. SLO vocabulary or massive metrics are not mandatory; operating behavior must be described.|
|Ownership/technical influence|15|Personal design-to-delivery ownership, decisions under ambiguity, cross-functional execution, adoption or mentoring. Title/employer prestige/tenure alone are insufficient.|

Released, maintained, adopted open source qualifies for 3/4 regardless of pay. Alpha/beta can qualify for 3 with real consumers/operation; maturity labels alone prove neither depth nor absence. Node.js/AWS loses no points solely for missing Deno/Bun/GCP/Azure. Equivalent non-MCP integration/auth gets capability credit. Frontend history does not erase direct relevant accomplishments.
